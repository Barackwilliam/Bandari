What we move, and how

Five services. One corridor.

DSM PORT

Ocean Freight Forwarding

FCL and LCL bookings in and out of Dar es Salaam, vessel scheduling, and bill-of-lading handling for cargo arriving from China, India, the UAE and Europe.

TANCIS

Customs Clearing

Licensed clearing agents working inside the TANCIS single window — duty assessment, inspection coordination and release, without the cargo sitting idle at the terminal.

KURASINI ICD

Bonded Warehousing

Transit and bonded storage near the port for cargo awaiting onward documentation, consolidation, or border-window timing.

TANZAM / TAZARA

Inland Haulage

Flatbeds, tautliners and tankers running the Tanzam Highway and TAZARA rail corridor to Mbeya, Tunduma, and across the Zambian and Malawian borders.

BORDER POSTS

Cross-Border Documentation

Transit bonds, COMESA carnets and border-post liaison at Tunduma, Kabanga, Mutukula and Rusumo, so drivers aren't the ones negotiating paperwork.

LAST MILE

Project & Heavy Cargo

Abnormal loads, mining equipment and construction machinery — permitting, route surveys and escort coordination for oversized freight.

Why shippers stay with one agent

The port is the easy part.
The border is where shipments stall.

Most delays on this route don't happen at sea — they happen at Tunduma, at Kabanga, at a weighbridge outside Mbeya. We keep one team on the file from vessel discharge to final delivery, so a hold-up at a border post is our problem to solve, not a phone call you have to make yourself.
