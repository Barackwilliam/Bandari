Sectors

Cargo looks different by industry. So does the routing.

Six sectors make up most of what moves through our network — each with its own paperwork, timing pressure and equipment needs.

Home / Industries
SECTOR

Mining & Minerals

Copper concentrate from the Zambian Copperbelt and DRC's Katanga belt moves through Dar es Salaam in both directions — raw material out, machinery and reagents in. Timing against vessel schedules matters more here than almost anywhere else.

SECTOR

Agriculture & Agro-Export

Cashew, coffee, tobacco and maize move both ways through the port — export cargo heading to global buyers, and agricultural inputs coming inland to farms across the corridor. Seasonality drives most of the planning here.

SECTOR

Construction & Energy

Steel, cement-related machinery and fuel-adjacent cargo, much of it requiring permitting before it ever leaves the port. We work directly with site logistics teams rather than just the importer of record.

SECTOR

Retail & FMCG

Consumer goods consolidated into LCL loads at the port, then broken down again inland for distribution. Speed through customs matters more than for almost any other cargo type — shelf life and demand timing don't wait.

SECTOR

Oil, Gas & Fuel

Fuel imports moving inland to Zambia, Malawi, Rwanda and Burundi via tanker truck and TAZARA bulk wagon — landlocked countries that depend on this port for a significant share of their supply.

SECTOR

NGO & Humanitarian Cargo

Relief supplies, medical cargo and project equipment for organisations operating in the Great Lakes region — usually time-sensitive and requiring careful documentation across multiple borders.
