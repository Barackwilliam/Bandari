CASE STUDY

Cutting Tunduma dwell time for a copper exporter

A Mbeya-based mineral exporter was losing two to three days per shipment to documentation delays at the Tunduma border post. We placed a resident agent at the post to pre-clear transit bonds before trucks arrived, and moved their cargo onto a fixed weekly TAZARA rail slot for volumes that didn't need road speed.

Before5–6 day average transit, Dar to Lusaka
After3–4 day average transit
ChangeResident border agent + fixed rail slot
CASE STUDY

Multi-border relief cargo for an NGO network

A relief organisation needed medical and shelter supplies split across destinations in Rwanda and Burundi from a single vessel arrival. We consolidated at the port, ran parallel customs files for each destination, and split the load onto two trucks routed through Isaka — cutting what had been a sequential process into one running in parallel.

CargoMedical supplies & shelter materials
DestinationsKigali & Bujumbura, same vessel
ResultParallel customs filing, single port handling
