What it's actually like to work here

Cargo doesn't stall in an office in Dar es Salaam — it stalls at a border post, a weighbridge, or a depot 1,800km from headquarters. We hire people who are comfortable solving problems out there, not just escalating them upward. In return, the people closest to the corridor get the most say in how it's run.
