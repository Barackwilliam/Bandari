SERVICE · DSM PORT

Ocean Freight Forwarding

We book and manage FCL and LCL shipments arriving at the Port of Dar es Salaam from China, India, the UAE and Europe, and outbound exports leaving through the same berths. That includes vessel scheduling, bill-of-lading handling, and shipping-line liaison so cargo isn't waiting on paperwork once it's discharged.

Container types20ft / 40ft / 40HC / reefer
ConsolidationLCL groupage twice weekly
DocumentationBill of lading, packing list, COO
SERVICE · TANCIS

Customs Clearing

Our clearing agents operate inside the Tanzania Customs Integrated System (TANCIS), the electronic single window used by the Tanzania Revenue Authority. We handle duty assessment, inspection coordination and terminal release on your behalf, working the file from the agent's desk rather than the dock.

SystemTANCIS electronic single window
Duty assessmentHS classification & valuation
InspectionScanning & physical exam coordination
SERVICE · KURASINI ICD

Bonded & Transit Warehousing

When cargo can't move onward immediately — waiting on a border window, a consignee's documentation, or rail-wagon availability — it goes into bonded or transit storage near the port rather than accruing demurrage at the terminal.

Storage typeBonded, transit & general cargo
LocationKurasini, within 4km of port gate
HandlingForklift, palletising, re-stuffing
SERVICE · TANZAM / TAZARA

Inland Haulage

Flatbeds, tautliners and tankers run the Tanzam Highway and the TAZARA rail corridor daily, carrying cargo to Mbeya, across the border at Tunduma, and on to Zambian and Malawian destinations. Rail is used for bulk cargo where road congestion or cost makes it the better option.

Road fleetFlatbed · tautliner · tanker · lowbed
Rail optionTAZARA bulk wagons, Dar – Kapiri Mposhi
Typical transit3–5 days, port to Lusaka
SERVICE · BORDER POSTS

Cross-Border Documentation

Transit bonds, COMESA carnets and direct liaison at the border posts our trucks actually cross — Tunduma, Kabanga, Mutukula and Rusumo — so the documentation is settled before the driver reaches the gate, not at it.

Border postsTunduma · Kabanga · Mutukula · Rusumo
PermitsTransit bonds, COMESA & SADC carnets
On-site liaisonResident agents at Tunduma & Kabanga
SERVICE · LAST MILE

Project & Heavy Cargo

Mining equipment, generators and construction machinery move under abnormal-load permits, with route surveys done ahead of time and escort vehicles arranged for anything outside standard dimensions.

Load typeAbnormal & out-of-gauge cargo
Pre-move workRoute survey & permit application
EscortPilot vehicle on request
