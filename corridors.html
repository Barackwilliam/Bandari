CORRIDOR A

Tanzam Highway & TAZARA Rail

The southern route — Dar es Salaam to Mbeya, across the border at Tunduma, into Zambia's Copperbelt and on to the Democratic Republic of Congo and Malawi. This is the corridor that was purpose-built for it: the Tanzania-Zambia Railway runs 1,860km from Dar es Salaam to Kapiri Mposhi, carrying copper exports out and fuel imports in, alongside the parallel Tanzam road route.

→ Zambia

Lusaka and the Copperbelt towns, via Tunduma. Primary cargo: copper concentrate out, fuel and consumer goods in.

→ DR Congo

Lubumbashi and the Katanga mining belt, transiting Zambia from Tunduma. Mining equipment and project cargo.

→ Malawi

Lilongwe and Blantyre via the Kyela border link near Mbeya. Tobacco exports and fuel imports.

CORRIDOR B

The Central Corridor

The northern and western route — Dar es Salaam through Dodoma and Isaka, reaching landlocked Rwanda, Burundi and Uganda. This corridor carries transit freight and passengers for Burundi, DR Congo and Rwanda heading to and from the Indian Ocean, and is the route a new standard-gauge railway is being built to serve.

→ Rwanda

Kigali via Isaka and the Rusumo border post. Manufactured goods in, coffee and tea out.

→ Uganda

Kampala via Mutukula. General cargo and agricultural inputs share this leg with regional bus traffic.

→ Burundi

Bujumbura via Kabanga. Smaller volumes, longer transit — usually consolidated with Rwanda-bound loads.
