FOUNDED 2014

A clearing desk that grew a fleet

Bandari Route started as a small customs clearing operation working the Kurasini gate at the Port of Dar es Salaam. Clients kept asking the same question after clearance: now what happens to it inland? In 2016 we bought our first three trucks to answer that question ourselves, rather than handing cargo to a haulier we couldn't vouch for at the border.

Today the fleet has grown past 180 units, we run a resident agent at two border posts, and the same questions clients asked in 2014 — what happens after clearance — are the reason the company exists in its current form.

Milestones

Eleven years on one corridor

2014

Customs clearing desk opens at Kurasini

Three staff, one client, working the port gate full time.

2016

First three trucks purchased

Began hauling cleared cargo to Mbeya directly, rather than subcontracting.

2018

Bonded warehouse opens near the port

Reduced terminal demurrage for clients waiting on inland documentation.

2020

Resident agent placed at Tunduma

Pre-clearing transit bonds before trucks reached the border post.

2022

Central Corridor route added — Rwanda, Uganda, Burundi

Second agent placed at Kabanga to cover the northern corridor.

2025

Fleet passes 180 units; TAZARA rail partnership formalised

Bulk cargo option added alongside road haulage for mineral and fuel clients.
